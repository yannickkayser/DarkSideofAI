Take your time. Speed is important, but quality is more important. First, learn to do excellent work. Then learn to excellent work quickly. Remember, only approved tasks result in payment. Submit everything prior to the project deadline. Late submissions might not be counted. If you’ve done some work but haven’t completed the project, and the deadline is approaching, consider submitting the portion you have completed. Need more help?
